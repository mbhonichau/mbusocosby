    Problem Statement

    Even though weather forecasts have improved, people in South Africa still struggle with water and sanitation during extreme weather like heavy rains and droughts. These challenges often lead to flooding, dirty water, and higher risks of diseases that spread through water.

    Innovative Solutions

    This project aims to create a system that uses machine learning to predict severe weather and understand how it affects water and sanitation systems. By using IBM’s tools and public data like past weather reports, water quality records, and infrastructure strength, the system will help manage water resources better. It will also provide real-time updates and predictions to prepare communities and make their water and sanitation systems more resilient.

    Project Objectives

    • Develop an ML System: Create predictive models using machine learning to forecast severe weather events.
    • Analyze Impact: Evaluate and quantify severe weather effects on local water availability and sanitation infrastructure.
    • Real-Time Community Alerts: Deliver actionable updates and early warnings to communities to reduce disease risk and flooding.
