    Healthcare Disease Detection & Resource Allocation Model

    ICD-10 Code Classification

    Problem Statement

    South Africa’s healthcare system faces critical challenges, including resource shortages, delayed outbreak responses, and inefficient resource allocation. Hospitals lack staff, beds, and medications, while patients in underserved areas struggle with limited access to care. Healthcare workers face burnout due to high workloads, and fragmented data systems hinder timely government responses to diseases like tuberculosis and COVID-19.

    Proposed Solution

    Early Disease Detection
    Predicts specific ICD-10 codes and medical conditions early based on symptom severity, patient history, and demographics.
    Resource Allocation Optimization
    Predicts required ICU beds, ventilator usage, and treatment costs to prevent hospital bottlenecks.

    Flask Web Application & API Simulation

    Power BI Analytics Dashboard

    Integrated executive dashboard for hospital administrators tracking capacity, outbreak trends, and cost efficiency:

    ICU Bed Occupancy Real-Time Forecast
    Disease Code Trends ICD-10 Distribution
    Treatment Cost Variance Budget Allocation

    Data Sources

    • Altron Medical Records: Anonymized clinical and patient history database.
    • OpenStreetMap GIS Data: Spatial data mapping regional healthcare facility access.
    • NICD COVID-19 Data: Communicable disease outbreak statistics.
    • Stats SA Demographics: Regional population metrics and risk stratification.

    Model Training & Architecture

    • Treatment Cost: RandomForestRegressor trained on scaled financial metrics.
    • Disease Classification: RandomForestClassifier mapping multi-label ICD-10 codes.
    • Resource Allocation: Binary classifier predicting ICU and Ventilator necessity.
    • Model Persistence: Serialized using joblib (.pkl binaries).
    • Backend Server: Flask microservice handling HTTP POST requests.

    Impact & Scalability

    Impact: Reduces healthcare worker burnout, improves clinical outcomes, and guarantees equitable resource distribution for rural & underserved populations.

    Scalability: Highly adaptable for regional expansion, diverse health datasets, and cloud deployment.

    Challenges & Considerations

    • Data Quality: Cleaned & preprocessed missing data using Pandas & StandardScaler.
    • Privacy & POPIA: Rigorous anonymization of patient records conforming to healthcare laws.
    • Cloud Infrastructure: High-availability deployment for real-time decision support.

    Study Objective & Introduction

    This study predicts individual employment status from 2012 to 2022 using socio-economic factors including age, education level, job search status, and social support grants. Utilizing a Random Forest Classifier, the project identifies key drivers of employment status and projects labor market trends into 2023.

    Random Forest Model & Evaluation

    High Accuracy Scikit-Learn Evaluated
    2023 Forecast Age Demographics

    Age, educational attainment, and reliance on social support grants were identified as the primary feature importance contributors influencing employment outcomes across South Africa.

    Strategic Policy Recommendations

    1. Focus on Age-Related Interventions: Formulate policies targeting age-specific employment trends, with special focus on vulnerable youth (<15 entry) and older demographics (>64).

    DIRISA Datathon (2024)

    Global Unemployment Rate Analysis & Prediction (1991 - 2022)

    Global 266-Country Forecasting Model, ARIMA, Random Forest & Power BI Analytics

    Global Unemployment Analysis (1991 - 2022)

    This study delves into the analysis and prediction of unemployment rates across 266 countries over 31 years (1991-2022). By examining historical economic trends, identifying key predictors (GDP, inflation, youth labor force ages 15-24, education), and modeling future trajectories, the project forecasts expected global unemployment rates for 2023.

    Methodology & Model Evaluation

    • Data Preprocessing: Data cleaning, outlier handling, feature selection, normalization using Pandas & StandardScaler.
    • Model Ensembles: Time Series Analysis (ARIMA), Linear Regression, Random Forest, and Microsoft Power BI Executive Dashboards.
    • Evaluation Metrics: Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Root Mean Square Error (RMSE), and R-squared ($R^2$).

    2023 Global Unemployment Forecast & Drivers

    The model predicts an expected global increase in unemployment rates for 2023 driven by three major international forces:

    • Post-Pandemic Recovery Adjustments: Economies stabilizing after COVID-19 disruptions face labor market realignment.
    • Inflationary Pressures: Global inflation triggering tighter monetary policies and reducing job creation rates.
    • Geopolitical Tensions: Regional conflicts and trade instability creating economic uncertainty across developing nations.

    Data Sources

    • World Bank: Global economic indicators, GDP growth, & inflation metrics.
    • International Labour Organization (ILO): Labor force & youth unemployment figures.
    • National Statistics Agencies: Country-specific employment reporting across 266 nations.

    Policy Suggestions

    • Economic Stimulus: Target job creation in high-growth sectors.
    • Education Alignment: Bridge technical skills with market demand.
    • International Cooperation: Mitigate geopolitical shockwaves on developing labor markets.

    Power BI Showcase

    Includes interactive Power BI visual analysis across all 266 countries, time series Matplotlib graphs, and model deployment code for economic policy planning.

    South African Temperature Data Analysis & Forecasting Model

    13-Year Climate Dataset (2011–2024), MATLAB Live Scripts, Time Series ML & 3-Day Temperature Forecasting

    June 2011–July 2024
    13-Year Historical Dataset
    30-Day Window

    Challenge Overview & Partners

    Brought to you by Opti-Num Solutions, MathWorks, and DIRISA, this challenge elevates technological literacy and climate data-driven decision-making in South Africa. Using real-world weather station readings from June 2011 to July 2024, the solution explores climate trends and deploys time series machine learning forecasting.

    Machine Learning Forecasting Architecture

    The model structure takes past environmental parameters to forecast future proceeding temperatures:

    • Input Window: Given a target date, the model processes the past 30 days of minimum and maximum daily temperature readings.
    • Output Prediction: The AI model predicts the minimum and maximum temperatures for the proceeding 3 days.
    • Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Deep dive analysis into seasonal cycles, heatwave trends, winter drop points, and station anomalies over 13 years.

    MATLAB Live Script & Artifact Deliverables

    The project submission is structured in MATLAB for full reproducible execution:

    • MATLAB Live Script (.mlx): Interactive code notebook integrating markdown reasoning, EDA graphics, model equations, and performance evaluation.
    • Workspace Binary File (.mat): Contains all trained model objects, feature matrices, and workspace variables.
    • MATLAB Drive Deployment: Shared workspace folder setup for instant cloud verification.

    MathWorks Training & Certification

    • MATLAB Onramp: Self-paced proficiency course completed via MathWorks Training Services.
    • Machine Learning Onramp: Specialized time series forecasting and classification modules.

    Model Performance & Enhancements

    Evaluated model performance using mean error margins against 13-year temperature bounds. Enhancements include integrating relative humidity, atmospheric pressure, and satellite spatial weather layers to refine 3-day predictive accuracy.
